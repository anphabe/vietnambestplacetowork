Survey method

The Vietnam Best Places to Work® survey, designed and conducted by Anphabe, records objective assessments from employees, aiming to measure the Employer Brand health of more than 700 leading businesses across 18 industries in Vietnam.

Measure Employer Brand Attractiveness

Top 100 Vietnam Best Places to Work (Large and Medium enterprises) is based on Employer Brand Attractiveness Index of each company, evaluated by respective industry's professional.

  • Awareness
  • Interest
  • Action
  • Desire
  • 1st choice

Ideal Workplace criteria

At the same time, for a more comprehensive assessment, Anphabe collected additional feedback from internal staff groups at companies (in need) according to the ideal working environment criteria framework with 6 major factors:

  • Rewards
  • Development Opportunities
  • Culture & Environment
  • Leadership & Management
  • Quality of Work & Life
  • Company Reputation
