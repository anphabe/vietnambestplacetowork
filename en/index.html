FAQs

What is the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey?

Launched in 2013, Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey is the leading in-depth and prestigious survey program on Employer Brand, organized annually by Anphabe, under the auspices of the Vietnam Chamber of Commerce and Industry (VCCI). The assessment model and method are verified by a third party, the market research company Intage Vietnam, to ensure fairness and objectivity.

The Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey is conducted completely independently, recording the most objective opinions from 60.000 employees looking to measure Employer Branding of more than 700 leading businesses, Top 18 occupations in Vietnam. The program has so far attracted millions of views, shares and reminders from leading press and television media units nationwide.

Each year, the survey provides valuable analysis on the trends of employees The latest work and work environment trends have always been considered a valuable and professional source of information for CEOs and HR leaders in orienting talent strategies.

Are businesses participating in the survey guaranteed to be included in the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Ranking?

Whether a company is included in the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Ranking or not depends entirely on the measurement results and votes from employees. This survey is conducted completely independently, recording completely objective assessments from employees. Based on those assessments, Anphabe will measure and calculate the Employer Brand Attractiveness Index of each business. As a result, only the 100 companies with the highest scores from top to bottom (by category) will have the opportunity to have their names listed on the Ranking. The model and measurement method of the Survey are also verified by a third party, the market research company Intage Vietnam, to ensure that the results are always objective and fair. Therefore, not all companies participating in the Survey will be honored in the Ranking.

When is the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey conducted?

The Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey is conducted annually by Anphabe, the specific time will be announced on the program's official media pages.

This year, the 11th season of the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey has officially launched and started accepting registration documents from businesses until March 31, 2024. The survey is expected to be launched from April to July 2024. The analysis and results after the survey will be announced in October 2024 at the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Summit 2024.
