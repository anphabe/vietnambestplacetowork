Vietnam Best Places To Work®

VIETNAM BEST PLACES TO WORK® is a prestigious annual award organized by Anphabe, a pioneer in Employer Branding and fostering a Happy Workforce.

This award and ranking are derived from the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey, which Anphabe conducts to gather objective evaluations and insights from tens of thousands of experienced working professionals. The survey assesses the Employer Brand Attractiveness of nearly 800 leading organizations across 18 key industries in Vietnam.

Since its inception in 2013, the program has been endorsed by the Vietnam Chamber of Commerce and Industry (VCCI). To ensure fairness and objectivity, the ranking methodology and results are validated by the third-party organization - Intage Vietnam.

FAQs

What is the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey?

Launched in 2013, the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey is a comprehensive and prestigious program focused on Employer Branding, organized annually by Anphabe, with the endorsement of the Vietnam Chamber of Commerce and Industry (VCCI). The evaluation model and methodology are validated by the third-party market research firm Intage Vietnam to ensure fairness and objectivity.

The Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey is conducted entirely independently, gathering objective feedback from an average of tens of thousands of employees employees nationwide each year, measuring the attractiveness of Employer Brands across nearly 800 leading companies in 18 different industry sectors in Vietnam. The program has attracted millions of views, shares, and mentions from top media outlets nationwide.

Each year, the survey provides valuable analyses of the latest trends among workers and workplace environments, and it is consistently regarded as a valuable and professional information source for CEOs and HR leaders in shaping talent strategies.

Do companies participating in the survey guaranteed to be listed in Vietnam Best Places to Work® Ranking?

Whether a company appears in the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Ranking depends entirely on the measurement results and ratings from Vietnam talents across industries. This survey is conducted by Anphabe in an independent role, capturing entirely objective evaluations from the workforce. Based on these assessments, Anphabe measures and scores the Employer Brand Attractiveness index for each company. As a result, only the top 100 companies with the highest scores (in each category) have the opportunity to be listed in the ranking. Therefore, not all companies participating in the survey will be honored in the ranking.

When is the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Survey conducted?

The Vietnam Best Places to Work® survey is conducted annually by Anphabe, with specific timelines announced across our official communication channels.

The Vietnam Best Places to Work® 2026 Survey (13th edition) has officially been launched and is now open for enterprise registration until March 31, 2026. The survey is expected to run from April to July 2026. All analysis and results will be unveiled in November 2026 at the Vietnam Best Places to Work® Summit 2026.
